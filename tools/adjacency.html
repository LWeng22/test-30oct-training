Adjacency scorer

Decide how close each pair of departments should be, place the departments on a grid, and see how well your layout follows those wishes.

How it works

Adjacent means two departments share a side on the grid. Corners touching does not count. Each pair of departments gets a closeness rating:

    Example: Receiving and Storage are rated A, Storage and Assembly are rated E, and Receiving and Assembly are rated X. Placing them in a row as Receiving, Storage, Assembly scores 1000 + 100 = 1100. Moving Assembly next to Receiving would instead lose 1000 points.
